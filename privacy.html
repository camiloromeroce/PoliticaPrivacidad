Fake Chat Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Fake Chat is an app developed by Camilo Romero for creating simulated conversations to use as a prank. This policy explains what data the app handles, what it does not, and who is responsible for it.

Fake Chat does not send real messages, does not connect to any messaging service and has no user accounts. The conversations you create are fictional, and you create them.

What you create stays on your phone

Conversations, the contacts you invent, groups, photos, stickers, voice notes and the wallpapers you pick are stored only in your device's internal storage. They are not uploaded to any server, neither the developer's nor a third party's.

The content of your conversations is never logged or transmitted. If you uninstall the app, all of it is deleted with it.

The only way that content leaves your phone is if you decide so: when you use the option to export a conversation as an image, you choose who to share it with, through your own device's apps.

Permissions the app requests

  • Microphone — only while you record a voice note. The audio is saved on the phone and is not sent anywhere.
  • Camera — your system camera app opens when you ask to take a photo for a conversation. The photo is saved on the phone.
  • Notifications — to show the simulated incoming call that you schedule yourself.
  • Exact alarms — so that simulated call rings at the time you chose. If you do not grant it, the app still works and the call may arrive late.
  • Vibration — for the simulated call.

The app does not access your real contacts, your real messages, your location or your calendar.

Data handled by third-party services

Google AdMob (advertising)

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may process device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID, and data about ad interactions, in order to serve ads and measure their performance. You can limit or reset that identifier from your device settings, under Google privacy options.

See how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Firebase Analytics (app usage)

Aggregate actions are logged to understand which features get used: for example, that a chat was created, that a message was sent, that a voice note was recorded or that a conversation was exported.

Message content is never logged, nor the names you invent, nor the photos. Only the fact that the action happened is measured.

Firebase Crashlytics (crashes)

If the app crashes, a technical report is sent with the error type, device model and Android version, so it can be fixed. That report does not include the content of your conversations.

Firebase Remote Config (visual settings)

The app downloads a handful of appearance settings from Google, such as the bubble corner radius or where the navigation bar goes. It is a download: the app asks for those values and sends nothing about you or about what is in your conversations in return.

Google Play Billing (purchases)

If you buy the option to remove ads, the purchase is processed by Google Play. The developer neither receives nor stores your payment details.

If you are in Europe (GDPR / UK GDPR)

If you use Fake Chat in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the party responsible for the data described here (the data controller) is Camilo Romero, and the following applies to you.

Your consent for advertising

The first time you open the app you are shown a consent form asking whether your data may be used for personalised advertising. Nothing is requested from the ad network until you answer it.

If you accept, the legal basis for that processing is your consent. If you refuse, the app still shows ads, but non-personalised ones: they are chosen without building a profile of you.

Changing your mind

You can review or withdraw your consent at any time from Ad preferences, in the menu of the app's main screen. That reopens the same form, and withdrawing is as easy as giving it was.

The rest of the processing

Crash reports and the usage measurement described above are processed on the basis of legitimate interest: keeping the app working and understanding which features are used. Neither includes the content of your conversations.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to data portability. In practice there is very little to request: what you create in Fake Chat never leaves your phone, so it is not held by anyone, and you remove it by deleting the conversation or uninstalling the app.

For anything concerning the data that third parties do process, write to the address in the Contact section. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of your country.

Children

Fake Chat is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children under that age.

Your choices

  • You can delete any conversation from within the app; its photos and audio are removed from the phone along with it.
  • You can uninstall the app to remove all local data.
  • You can reset or delete the advertising identifier from your device settings.
  • If you are in Europe, you can change or withdraw your advertising consent from Ad preferences, in the menu of the app's main screen.

Changes to this policy

If what the app handles changes, this page will be updated and the date in the header will change.

Contact

The party responsible for the data described here is Camilo Romero. For anything related to this policy or to privacy in Fake Chat, write to app.dev.android.7@gmail.com.
